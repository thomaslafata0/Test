Cinder Cell privacy

Updated 5 November 2026

Cinder Cell has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It does not collect your name, email, contacts or location.

What stays on your device

Your game save, sound settings and co-op name are kept on your phone or in your browser.

What goes to our server

  • Cloud save. A copy of your game save is stored under a random cell code, so you can load it on another device. It holds only game data. Anyone with the code can load the save, so keep the code private.
  • Leaderboard. Each operative's in-game name, class, level and how far they got are shown on the public leaderboard.
  • Co-op. When you host or join a room, game moves and the name you chose for co-op are passed between the two players. They are not stored.

The server runs on Cloudflare, which may keep standard network logs such as IP addresses for security.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Removing your data

Use Settings, Reset save in the game to erase the save on your device. To have a cloud save or leaderboard entry removed, contact the developer through TestFlight feedback or the App Store listing.
